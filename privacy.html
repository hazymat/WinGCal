Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

WinGCal runs on your PC and talks directly to Google. The developer has no server, and never sees, collects, stores or shares any of your data. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Who we are

WinGCal is a free, open-source Windows app for showing your Google calendars on the desktop. It is made by Mat (hazymat on GitHub), an independent developer in London, UK (“we”, “us”). The source code is public at github.com/hazymat/WinGCal, so anyone can check that it does what this policy says.

The Google data WinGCal uses, and why

When you sign in, Google asks you to allow WinGCal to use these, and WinGCal uses each one only for the feature described:

  • Your Google account’s e-mail address (OpenID Connect: openid, email). To label each account in the app and keep your accounts apart.
  • Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar). To read your list of calendars and their events so the widget can show them. WinGCal only adds, changes or deletes an event when you do that yourself in the app.
  • Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks), only if you turn on Tasks in Settings. To show your task lists beside the calendar. WinGCal only adds, ticks off or deletes a task when you do that yourself in the app.

WinGCal doesn’t ask for, or use, any other Google data.

How your Google data is used

  • It is used only to provide the features you see in WinGCal on your own PC.
  • It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. It travels only between your PC and Google, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to build profiles of you, and not read by any person.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

WinGCal’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Everything WinGCal keeps is stored on your PC, in your own Windows user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinGCal-data, or next to the app if you use the portable version):

  • Sign-in tokens, encrypted with Windows’ Data Protection so only your Windows account can read them. WinGCal never sees or stores your Google password: you sign in on Google’s own page in your browser.
  • A copy of your calendars and events for the range you choose, so the widget opens instantly and works offline.
  • Your settings, such as which calendars show, the skin and the window position.
  • Export files, only when you choose to export, saved where you choose.

Other connections

  • Updates: WinGCal checks GitHub for new versions of itself. GitHub sees an ordinary web request from your PC (such as your IP address), as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. No calendar data is included.
  • Links you click: opening an event’s location or video link opens it in your web browser (for example, Google Maps or Google Meet).

Removing your data

  • Sign out of an account in Settings › Accounts. WinGCal tells Google to revoke its access, and deletes that account’s sign-in and its copy of the events from your PC.
  • You can also remove WinGCal’s access at any time from your Google Account, at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To remove everything, uninstall WinGCal and delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinGCal-data folder.

As we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

WinGCal is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. The history of every change is public in the site’s source.
